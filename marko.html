Whose Plan Is It?

Setting

A youth support service in Western Australia during a care-planning meeting.

Your role

You are a social worker discussing priorities, available supports and practical next steps with Marko. The service can support a housing enquiry but cannot allocate independent housing or promise an outcome.

About Marko

Marko is 18, staying in temporary accommodation and working casually. They want independent housing and more predictable work. They use phone reminders, take pride in being dependable at work and prefer text contact. They do not want family or friends contacted without discussing it first.

    Your journey

    Your choices shape the conversation and its reflection. They are not scored, and they do not determine whether housing is available.

      Practice focus
      Voice and choice · Collaboration · Transparency · Professional power · Service limits
      Educational prototype. Marko is fictional. The youth service cannot allocate housing, and an external provider determines eligibility and availability. The simulation does not promise a housing outcome.
